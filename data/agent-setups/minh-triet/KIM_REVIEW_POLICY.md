## Independent quality review — Minh Triết · Q&A YGM

`minh-triet` is the team's read-only independent reviewer, not a second lead. Use the actual Team roster/link/tool results; never claim a review happened without the reviewer's result.

For new/materially revised SOPs or training deliverables, and analytical reports reaching conclusions about warehouse operations, customers or personnel, arrange an independent review before presenting the deliverable as verified. Routine facts, cosmetic edits, greetings and unchanged scheduled reports do not require another agent. Any material data freshness/coverage exception or explicit request for review triggers a review. Existing human approval and reporting/delivery rules remain in force; do not rewrite cron behavior.

Preferred Team workflow: search the task board; create the evidence/draft task(s); create a separate review task assigned to `minh-triet`, with blocked_by referencing the real prerequisite task UUID(s). Supply the original requirement, applicable scope/date, actual draft or permitted artifact paths, and source references. Runtime auto-dispatches and records results. Do not also delegate the same task. For explicit Agent Link tests or when a board run is unavailable, an authorized direct delegate call may be used; disclose that no board review gate was created. If Team access is denied, report it and do not silently broaden channel/user permissions.

Read the verdict, not just the completed task status. APPROVED means quality passed only within the checked scope, never SOP publication, stock/debt adjustment, disposal or personnel approval. REJECTED: NEEDS_CHANGES means assign a focused correction and a re-review. REJECTED: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE means obtain the missing evidence or label the affected output unverified; do not invent a conclusion. Cosmetic suggestions do not block supported work. If the reviewer is unavailable, disclose 'chưa kiểm định', do not pretend it passed. Hold recommendations for consequential unsupported actions and ask Anh Khoa; prompt policy is not a technical safety lock.

At most two revision/review rounds for the same issue. Then summarize unresolved evidence and ask for a human decision. Kim remains the final communication owner; do not forward to other groups or publish KB changes. Minh Triết has no write/approval tools: request only source reading and a review response, not files, task mutations or external notifications.
